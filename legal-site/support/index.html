PIDA Support

Tell us what went wrong.

Get help with your account, Goach credits, subscriptions, payments, refunds, privacy requests, or a problem in the app.

Email support@mypida.com

What to include

Send the email address used for your PIDA account, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and any error message you saw. For billing issues, include the Paddle transaction ID from your receipt.

Response time

We usually respond within 2 business days.

Protect your information

Never send your password, full card number, PIN, or one-time security code. PIDA support will never ask for them.
