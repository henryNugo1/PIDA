PIDA goal planning app

PIDA helps you turn intention into daily action.

PIDA is a goal planning and routine tracking app. It helps users set personal goals, create daily shifts, follow reminders, review progress, and use Goach, an AI goal coach, to quickly turn an idea into a clear plan they can act on.

What PIDA Does

PIDA helps people build useful routines from their goals. Users can create goal cards, add time shifts, receive reminders, review completed or missed shifts, and track their progress over time.

Goach is the AI goal coach inside PIDA. It helps users understand what they want to achieve and turns that intent into a realistic action plan.
